# Perf round 5 — four parallel workers, one GPU architecture each

Round 4 (`2026-09-12-perf-round4.md`, 17 accepted steps, −45 to −72 % on the official suite on a
5090) ran one worker at a time on one local GPU, because measurements contend. This round buys its
way out of that constraint: **four workers, four GPUs, four disjoint subsystems** — and turns the
fleet into a second deliverable, because the same change is measured on four architectures.

- **Base**: `perf-round5-base` = `890482c2` (round-4 head, after the 5090 acceptance A/B).
- **Brief**: `/workspace/output/round5/AGENT_BRIEF.md` (invariant commands, gates, ownership map).
- **Branches**: `perf/round5-<worker>` per worker, merged into `perf/round5` by the coordinator.
- **Budget**: $5 hard cap, enforced by `tools/vast/vast_budget_guard.sh` (destroys the whole fleet
  when account credit falls below $3.20), plus a 300-minute age backstop.

Rented boxes each hold their **own** baseline: `baseline_tests.txt` (correctness gate reference) and
`src/output/baseline-runs/*.json` (one benchmark run per scene at the base commit). A worker's gates
compare against its own box, never against another box's numbers.

## Assignment (from the post-s17 pick list, round-4 record §"Candidates after s17")

| Worker | Target | Why it was picked | Expected transfer to the 5090 |
|---|---|---|---|
| w0-turing | **`make_spd<N>`**, the PSD projection eigen-solve — replace Eigen's `SelfAdjointEigenSolver::compute()` for 4≤N≤9 with a fixed-sweep cyclic Jacobi (relative off-diagonal threshold) | measured biggest single cost centre of round 4: 82 % of the discrete-shell hinge kernel (2.14 ms/Newton-it on cube-wall, 15.8 % of its kernel time), 69 % of the ABD ortho potential, plus the tail of every contact branch. One function, four call sites | mostly algorithmic (dynamic-size Eigen internals and out-of-line calls) → should transfer; the register-pressure part will not transfer evenly |
| w1-ampere | **The 40 % of `StableNeoHookean3D` G/H that is not the stencil loop**: `qr_svd`, 3×3 `evd`, `SNH::dEdF`, `project_F_gradient`, ~1 450 FP64 per tet | largest untouched single kernel of the suite (case2 4.55 ms/launch = 13.9 %, bunny 2.32). An s04-style stage-stubbing probe first, to say which stage dominates before anything is rewritten | FP64-heavy: a 1/32-rate part over-rewards this. **Must be re-measured on w3 before acceptance** |
| w2-ada | **D2H round trips**: 24–30 blocking reads per Newton iteration, 1.3–3.0 ms/frame of GPU idle on *every* scene (s14 gap attribution) | the only candidate that pays on all four scenes at once; pure pipeline work, no kernel arithmetic touched | algorithmic/latency → transfers, and the relative win *grows* on a faster GPU (same host latency, less GPU work to hide it) |
| w3-blackwell | **Contact G+H part 2 (PE+PP)** launch config / register pressure (255 regs, 2 096 B frame, one 256-thread block per SM for a 9×9/6×6 projection), plus **`ortho_potential` + `abd_diag_preconditioner` on two streams** (3–8 blocks each, independent within a Newton iteration) | part 2 is 12.4 % of wrecking-balls kernel time and the contact family is its top two entries (28 %). s17 showed `__launch_bounds__` on part 2 wins −3.4 % alone but loses via the shared-SM fork — the newest architecture is the one that can answer this | occupancy tuning is the **least** transferable category; deliberately placed on the cc 12.0 box, closest to the acceptance target |

Ownership is enforced: a worker that finds the real cost in another worker's area reports it for
reassignment rather than fixing it there (brief §Ownership).
