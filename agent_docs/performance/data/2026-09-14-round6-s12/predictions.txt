Step: s11's candidate 1 -- the ABD body-local G/H prepass (s10/s11), applied to FEM.

What the brief asked me to verify first (PERF_METHOD 2.11): that the FEM elastic G/H is
"body-local in the same sense the ABD ortho potential is -- it depends on positions, not
on the contact set".

FINDING BEFORE ANY MEASUREMENT (code, not inference):
  The *inputs* are hoistable, exactly as the brief says.  The elastic G/H kernels read
  fem().xs, x_bars, Dm3x3_invs, rest_volumes, the tet/tri index buffers and the per-element
  material parameters; none of those is written by the contact phase.
  The *outputs* are not.  The ABD prepass works because its four outputs are PRIVATE
  buffers allocated once in Impl::init().  The FEM reporter gradient half is private
  (FEMLinearSubsystem::Impl::reporter_gradients) but the Hessian half is written straight
  into `info.hessians()`, a subview of the GLOBAL triplet_A, and three separate properties
  of that destination make it unavailable before the contact phase:
    (1) its SIZE includes dytopo_effect_receiver->hessians().triplet_count()
        (fem_linear_subsystem.cu:289) and triplet_A is only resized -- and, on growth,
        REALLOCATED -- at global_linear_system.cu:856-858, after the contact count exists;
    (2) its OFFSET is subsystem_triplet_offsets[triplet_i] + kinetic_count
        (global_linear_system.cu:920), which on any multi-subsystem scene includes a
        preceding subsystem's contact triplets;
    (3) it is OVERWRITTEN afterwards: _assemble_linear_system opens with
        triplet_A.row_indices().fill(-1) / col_indices().fill(-1)
        (global_linear_system.cu:881-882), on the default stream, after the contact phase.
  So the only surviving design is ABD's shape: stage the elastic Hessian in a private
  triplet buffer during the prepass, then COPY it into info.hessians() once that exists.
  ABD needs no such copy: its assemble_kinetic_shape_k1/k2 scatter already existed.

   Newton iteration against a 28.1 ms Newton iteration.  I predict the ceiling of ANY

   lower still, because s09 measured that hiding part 2 under part 1 costs part 1
   +3.83 % of its own time on this scene and the FEM elastic kernel is 40x part 2's grid.

P2 (why the ABD mechanism cannot repeat).  ortho_potential is 18 blocks of 32 threads and
   332 us against contact part 1's 1440 us -- it fits ENTIRELY inside part 1's shadow.
   StableNeoHookean3D G/H on case2 is 1249 blocks of 128 threads at 168 reg (3 blocks/SM
   on cc 7.5 -> 120 blocks fills the machine, so 10.4 waves) and 2829 us against part 1's
   880 us.  It is a saturating throughput kernel, not a latency-bound sliver.  I predict
   that at most 31 % of it (880/2829) can ever be concurrent with contact part 1, and that
   the two cannot share an SM at all: 128x168 = 21504 registers + part 1's 65280 exceeds
   the 65536-register file.

P3 (floor of the cost -- what this step measures).  The staging copy moves the reporter
   Hessian region twice: row indices (4 B) + col indices (4 B) + the 3x3 block (72 B) per
   triplet, read once and written once.  The FEM reporter Hessian region on case2 is
   ~1.7-1.8 M triplets (inferred: FEMLinearSubsystem_assemble_k2 runs over a median
   1902x1024 = 1.95 M triplets; kinetic is ~42 k and the dytopo region ~136 k).  That is
   ~280 MB per Newton iteration.  Two rates bracket it:
     - a pure contiguous D2D memcpy at ~380 GB/s  -> ~0.74 ms per Newton = +2.6 % of wall;
     - the tree's own triplet copy kernel rate: FEMLinearSubsystem_assemble_dytopo_effect_k2
       regresses at 2.31 ns per triplet over 332 launches (intercept -5.6 us)
       -> ~4.1 ms per Newton = +14.6 % of wall.
   I predict the measured probe (which is the memcpy form, i.e. the cheap end) lands at
   +2 to +4 % of case2's wall, and therefore that the copy alone exceeds the ENTIRE
   ceiling from P1.  VERDICT PREDICTED: the FEM G/H prepass is a net loss and s11's
   candidate 1 should be closed.
   If instead the probe reads below +1.5 %, P1's ceiling and P3's floor overlap and the
   design would need building and measuring properly rather than closing.

P4 (counts).  The probe writes only to scratch nothing reads, so no computed quantity
