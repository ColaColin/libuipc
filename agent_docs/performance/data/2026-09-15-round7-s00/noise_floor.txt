The workload is BIMODAL, unlike the tumbler: ~47 of 130 frames are press/hold/
lift/shift frames at 400-1200 ms (dense contact, 4-13 Newton), the settle tail runs
at 40-80 ms (1-2 Newton).  Consequences for later steps:
- medianFrameMs is WORTHLESS here (cv 20.6%): the median falls somewhere in the
  bimodal gap and jumps between arms.  Use meanFrameMs or ms_per_newton.
- ms_per_newton (cv 2.69%) is again the tightest statistic, but it is only
  legitimate when the change does not move Newton counts (which move 2.6% here
  on their own -- report both and the counts).
- the press phases carry the target kernels; a bending-kernel win will show up
  in meanFrameMs at n=5 only above ~5.5%.  Sub-2% work must use the per-kernel
  scope gate plus cube-wall-cloth / mas-bunny as the precision instruments.
