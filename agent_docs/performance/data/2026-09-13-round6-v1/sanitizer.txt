* **memcheck is clean on both scenes.**  Nothing round 6 added reads or writes out of
  bounds on either the tumbler or cube-wall-cloth.
* **initcheck**: every one of the 100 displayed findings, on both revisions and both
  scenes, is a 4-byte uninitialised `__global__` read inside
  `cub::DeviceScanKernel`, reached from `DeviceScan::ExclusiveSum` in
  `GlobalDyTopoEffectManager::Impl::_distribute`.  The cube-wall-cloth count is
  **identical to the digit on head and base (3483 / 3483)**.  This is the defect
  round 5's validation pass already diagnosed and recorded (the `N == 0` case:
  `loose_resize(selected_hessian, N+1)` sizes the buffer to 1 and the `if(n > 0)`
  guard skips the kernel that writes it, so `ExclusiveSum` scans one never-written
  int).  `global_dytopo_effect_manager.cu` is not in round 6's diff.
* **racecheck**: 0 errors everywhere; every warning is in a `MASPreconditionerEngine_*`
  kernel.  The per-kernel breakdown on cube-wall-cloth is **identical on head and
  base**: build_multi_level_R 50, prefix_sum_Lx 12, build_connect_mask_Lx 8,
  next_level_cluster 6, build_level1 4, prepare_prefix_sum_L0 2.  The tumbler adds
  10 more in build_multi_level_R and nothing new in kind.
  `finite_element/mas_preconditioner_engine.cu` is not in round 6's diff, and **no
  kernel round 6 touched appears in any hazard** -- in particular neither
  `DiscreteShellBending_do_compute_gradient_hessian_kernel` (s01, s02) nor
  `do_assemble_kernel` (s03).

Limits: 6 / 4 / 2 frames is a short window.  Sanitized runs are 100-1000x slower, so a
full 180-frame tumbler run under racecheck is hours; these windows do not cover the
dense-pile steady state that starts around frame 15, and they do not cover
`uipc_test_sim_case`.
