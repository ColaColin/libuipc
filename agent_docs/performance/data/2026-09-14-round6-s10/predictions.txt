  R4 (w3-blackwell, round 5) rejected "the abd_diag_preconditioner side stream from round 3"
  because "the trace shows the fork and join adjacent with no concurrent work between them".
  That side stream is still in the tree and still DEFAULT ON (UIPC_ABD_DIAG_SIDE_STREAM,
  abd_diag_preconditioner.cu, m_side = true). Confirmed on s09's rwb a2 trace: the assemble runs
  on stream 13 for 361 us and the default stream resumes 2.3 us after it ends. R4 stands.
  It is NOT fixable: diag_hessian is written by assemble_kinetic_shape_k2 AND accumulated into by
  the contact-derived reporter/dytopo-pair kernels (abd_linear_subsystem.cu:153, :277, :627), so
  the ABD diagonal preconditioner genuinely depends on the contact assembly. abd_diag cannot be
  hoisted under contact part 1. That half of s09's candidate is dead on the data flow.
  The OTHER half is alive and was never measured: ortho_potential_compute_gradient_hessian (and
  the bdf1 kinetic G/H beside it) read only qs/q_prev/q_tilde/masses/kappas/volumes and write only
  body_id_to_shape_{gradient,hessian} / body_id_to_kinetic_{gradient,hessian}, which nothing
  reads until assemble_kinetic_shape_k1/k2. They are hoistable.

TRANSFER
  Grid-against-machine-size (transfers and GROWS): the shadow exists because part 1's grid is 24
  blocks on 40 SMs. On a 170-SM part it is 24 of 170 and the shadow is far larger, so more ABD work
  fits. Occupancy half (255 reg = 1 block/SM): also grows, since cc 8.9/12.0 have 48 warp slots.
  Counter-pressure: faster FP64 shortens part 1 (10 563 FP64 instructions) and therefore shortens
  the shadow, while ortho's own 9x9 eigen-decomposition also shortens. Net: predict the gain
  survives and grows in absolute SM-idle terms but shrinks slightly as a percentage if ortho
  speeds up more than part 1 does.
