WHY: the prepass is issued to the GPU *ahead of* contact part 1, so the work distributor
places ortho's 18 single-warp blocks first. At 192 reg x 32 threads they cost 6 144 of an SM's
65 536 registers -- and part 1's block needs 65 280 of them (256 threads x 255 reg). One ortho
warp therefore makes an SM unusable for part 1 for ortho's whole duration. Measured: part 1
starts 567 us later than its fork event allows and ends 2.3 us after ortho does.
The assembly critical path shortens by 411 us/iteration and the wall does not move at all,
because the 411 us were simply moved in front of part 1 instead of under it.

FIX TO TEST: keep the fork event where it is (before the contact phase, which is what makes
the side stream independent of part 1's join) but ISSUE the prepass kernels *after*
compute_dytopo_effect() has issued part 1 and part 2. Then part 1's blocks are dispatched
first and ortho fills what part 1 leaves, which is the shadow this step is aiming at.
