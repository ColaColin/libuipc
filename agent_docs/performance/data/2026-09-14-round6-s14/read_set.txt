WHAT THE SIDE STREAM WRITES (part 1, stream 14; in mode 2 also part 2 on its own stream):
  the PT/EE (mode 2: + PE/PP) gradient and Hessian sub-views that SimplexNormalContact::do_assemble
  carves out of GlobalDyTopoEffectManager's `collected_dytopo_effect_gradient` / `_hessian`
  (simplex_normal_contact.cu:99-160: info.gradients().subview(...) / info.hessians().subview(...)).
  Nothing else: the kernels' other arguments are inputs (positions, rest positions, pair lists,
  thicknesses, d_hats, the contact tabular, m_ee_perm).

WHAT THE SIDE STREAM READS that somebody later WRITES (the R->W half of the hazard):
  positions            -> written by the line search's step (after solve) and next frame
  PTs/EEs pair lists   -> rewritten by the trajectory filter in the line search / next iteration
  m_ee_perm/m_ee_counters -> rewritten by the next do_assemble's partition_ee
  collected_* buffers  -> resized/reallocated by the next _assemble's loose_resize_entries
  All of these are ordered after `solve()`, whose assembly joins (below), or after the next
  do_assemble / _assemble, which join at their top.

THE STRUCTURAL GUARANTEE: the only way to obtain a device view of the received data is through a
receiver accessor that joins; the manager's own two readers join explicitly; the two remaining
direct readers (the exporters, the next assemble) join explicitly. A consumer that was missed would
show up as a NEW accessor, not as a silent race -- and `hessian_count()`-style host reads are the
only non-joining accessors, by name.
