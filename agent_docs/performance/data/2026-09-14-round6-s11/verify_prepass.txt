s11 -- bit-identity of the two new call sites, and of the shipped default
=========================================================================

UIPC_ABD_GH_PREPASS_VERIFY=1. After the join, the four prepass output buffers
(body_id_to_{shape,kinetic}_{gradient,hessian}) are snapshotted D2D, the SAME
work is recomputed in place on the default stream, and every 64-bit word of the
two copies is compared on device (s10's harness, unchanged).

The verifier stages its snapshot and its comparison kernel on the DEFAULT stream
and is called only after `_join_prepass()` has made the default stream wait on
the side stream's join event, so it does see the side-stream launch. (This is
the s20/s24 trap s10 recorded: a verifier on the default stream cannot see a
launch that ran elsewhere unless the join precedes it. Here it does.)

  mode 3 (between contact part 1 and part 2)
    rigid-wrecking-balls, 40 frames    20 416 032 words,  0 mismatching
    cube-wall-cloth,      40 frames   106 030 080 words,  0 mismatching

  mode 4 (just after contact part 2)  -- the new default
    rigid-wrecking-balls, 40 frames    20 595 120 words,  0 mismatching
    cube-wall-cloth,      40 frames   106 030 080 words,  0 mismatching

  shipped default, UIPC_ABD_GH_PREPASS unset (resolves to 4)
    rigid-wrecking-balls, 40 frames    20 595 120 words,  0 mismatching
    cube-wall-cloth,      40 frames   106 030 080 words,  0 mismatching

  ----------------------------------------------------------------------
  total                              379 696 512 words,  0 mismatching

This is the strong form. The side stream's only ordering constraint is the fork
event, and that event is recorded at the SAME point (before the dytopo-effect
phase) in modes 1, 2, 3 and 4 -- only the host's enqueue point moves, so the
GPU-visible dependency graph is identical across all of them. The four output
buffers have exactly one writer each (`_compute_kinetic_shape`) and exactly one
reader each (`assemble_kinetic_shape_k1/k2`), verified by grep at this head:
nothing in the contact or dytopo path names any of them, and there is no atomic
anywhere in the body-local gradient/Hessian. The join precedes every reader.

Because nothing downstream can see a difference, Newton / PCG / line-search
counts CANNOT be moved by this change, and any drift between arms is the scene's
own non-determinism -- which is why ms_per_newton is the legitimate statistic in
results.txt. The counts bear it out: on rigid-wrecking-balls at n=30 no arm's
Newton count differs from `off` by more than 0.33 % (all p > 0.33), and on the
deterministic mas-bunny every one of 18 runs reports Newton 465.

See sass_identity.txt for the compile-level half of the proof.
