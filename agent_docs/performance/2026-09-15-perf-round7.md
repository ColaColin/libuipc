# Perf round 7 — the constitutions no benchmark ever ran: dahl friction + plastic bending

Rounds 3–6 tuned the official four plus the round-6 tumbler. The TODO's own audit (PERF_METHOD §4,
"helpers that never reached their call sites") named the three shell-bending constitutions that **no
benchmark scene executes**: `DahlFrictionDiscreteShellBending` (zero callers in the repo at all; used
by `cloth-dataset`/`cloth-machine` outside it), `StrainPlasticDiscreteShellBending` and
`StressPlasticDiscreteShellBending` (C++ sim_case correctness tests only). A same-day coverage
survey confirmed only 5 of ~25 constitution families have benchmark callers, and that these three
kernels are the least-optimized in the bending family:

- the plain hinge ships `<Proj = 3 Gauss-Newton, Solver = 1 QL>` (round 6 s02/s19: −90 % kernel,
  −12 to −19 % end-to-end);
- dahl still launches with **runtime bools** and `Solver = 0` — Eigen's `SelfAdjointEigenSolver`,
  11 704 B stack frame, 255 registers, the largest in the family;
- the plastic variants call the bare dense `make_spd(H12x12)` — the +81.0 % / +19.4 % levers
  measured on the plain hinge's dense path apply to them verbatim.

Round 7 changes the workload again, in the TODO's own suggested shape: a **cyclic press creasing a
mixed stack** — denim (dahl), cardboard (strain-plastic), sheet metal (stress-plastic) — so those
kernels carry real benchmark load, then spends ten sequential steps wiring the proven hinge
machinery into the call sites that never had it. `NeoHookeanShell2D`, also unrepresented, is the
membrane for the carton/metal sheets.

- **Base**: tag `perf-round7-base` = `fce57589` (samples `2e52a52`).
- **Brief**: `/workspace/output/round7/AGENT_BRIEF.md`.
- **Box**: local RTX 2070 SUPER only (cc 7.5, 8 GB, FP64 1/32). **Serial**: one worker at a time.
- **Branches**: `perf/round7-sNN-<area>`, one step per branch cut from head at spawn, merged by the
  coordinator, step numbers assigned centrally at merge.
- **No acceptance box.** Every number here is cc 7.5; each accepted step records a transfer
  prediction (PERF_METHOD §6) a future rented run can falsify.

## Survey findings that fixed the scene choice (2026-09-15)

Constitution coverage matrix and the full unrepresented-parts ranking live in
`data/2026-09-15-round7-survey/`. Decisions taken from it:

1. **The scene targets the three plastic/frictional bending constitutions** — the only family where
   the TODO could quote measured headroom (+81.0 % dense-solve, +19.4 % `Solver=0`, −90 % GN
   analogue) with no scene to spend it on.
2. `NeoHookeanShell2D` is the membrane for the carton/metal sheets — a second unrepresented kernel
   exercised at no extra scene cost. Denim keeps `StrainLimitingBaraffWitkinShell`, matching the
   dahl users in `cloth-dataset`.
3. **Legacy broad-phase filters** (`lbvh`, `stackless_bvh`, `info_stackless_bvh_v0`): runtime-
   selectable via `collision_detection/method`, no benchmark sets it, one example (33) does. Round-6
   s04/s06 never reached them, but there is no benchmark justification to optimize them either —
   the TODO item is answered "reachable but unloaded; not this round" rather than deleted.
4. **Pipeline-level gaps** (AL-IPC contact constitution, ABD joints, BDF2, kirchhoff rods, vertex
   stitches, plain PCG) are future workload candidates, not one scene's payload — changing them
   changes what the scene *is*.

## s00 — the instrument

Scene: `libuipc-samples/examples/103_crease_press/` (next free number after
`102_net`). Evidence: `agent_docs/performance/data/2026-09-15-round7-s00/`.

**What it is.** A cyclic hydraulic press creasing a mixed stack of seven clamped
sheets — 3 denim (`StrainLimitingBaraffWitkinShell` + `DahlFrictionDiscreteShellBending`,
the cloth-dataset towel values verbatim: kappa 2e-5, ell 0.3, m_hat 8e-3, 0.8 mm,
0.30 kg/m², finest mesh), 2 cardboard (`NeoHookeanShell` + `StrainPlasticDiscreteShellBending`)
and 2 sheet metal (`NeoHookeanShell` + `StressPlasticDiscreteShellBending`),
101_press values with the yield thresholds carried over as the same yield
*curvature* (0.02 rad on 50 mm elements → 0.006 rad on 15.5 mm; the stress
threshold 250 stays verbatim and lands at the same per-hinge angle). The denim
is interleaved through the stack. One kinematic ABD press bar (cube +
`SoftTransformConstraint` + animator, 101_press's smooth schedule) runs **two
press/hold/lift cycles at two lateral offsets** (z = ±80 mm) with a lateral
shift between them, so creases form in two places and the dahl hinges run
hysteresis loops; the ground halfplane is the die that arrests the fold.
Everything is procedural; no new binary assets. 130 frames at dt = 1/60
(calibrated down from 101_press's 0.01 — at dt 0.01 the soft denim needs >10
Newton iterations per press frame and the run costs 3x the wall budget).

**Size.** 47,569 bending hinges (28,557 dahl / 9,506 strain-plastic / 9,506
stress-plastic), 32,168 triangles, 16,774 vertices; 35-39 s per 130-frame run
(n=5 mean 37.2 s), peak 2.47-2.72 GiB GPU (nvidia-smi total delta — the brief's
< 2.5 GB target, met at the 2.5 GiB line). `size_sweep.md` records the
dt/tolerance sweep, the element-size table (the carton edge is bounded below by
the contact-resolution rule applied to its 2.5 mm one-sided thickness: 14.2 mm
minimum), and the **five press-geometry drafts** it took to make offset creases
on a clamped stack work at all — four traps for the next scene of this kind:
no die under an offset crease → the yielded fold swings past vertical; die +
deep press → the clamp-corner triangles of the stiff carton shear to 30x rest
area along the sqrt(span²+depth²) membrane draw; kiss-press → a broad elastic
dome that never yields (the carton's bending boundary layer is metres wide);
die too shallow → contained but dead. The shipped geometry (die 75 mm under the
rest plane, face travel 0.10 m below the stack's rest top, sheet 0.48x0.80 m)
is the combination that localises the kink without wrecking the corners.

**Regime (mandatory check 2), measured by replicating the engine's per-frame
commit kernels in numpy** (`verify.py` tracks dahl `F_commit` with the exact
device update and both plastic `theta_bar` laws, hinge set for hinge set — the
hinge census of a flat grid sheet is its interior edges):

| observable | value |
|---|---|
| dahl `F_commit` non-zero fraction after press 1 | 0.9996 of hinges |
| dahl \|F\|/M at the crease lines, final | 0.064-0.067 of saturation |
| stress-plastic hinges holding a permanent `theta_bar` | 2.2-3.4 % |
| strain-plastic hinges holding a permanent `theta_bar` | 0.04-0.13 % (the kink peaks) |
| residual crease after retract, per sheet (7 sheets) | 37-48 mm on **every** sheet |

The strain fraction is small because the crease kink concentrates on 2-4 hinges
per crease line while the audit's yield fraction is over all 9,506 hinges; the
kernels' load does not depend on it (same launch geometry either way).

## s01 — the plastic-bending PSD projection (pick #1)

Evidence: `agent_docs/performance/data/2026-09-15-round7-s01/` (README maps every
file). Branch `perf/round7-s01-plastic-proj` from `fa99082b`.

What changed: the two plastic G/H kernels get the plain hinge's dispatch verbatim
— `template <int Proj, int Solver>`, six instantiations per file (the knob-reachable
set, like the hinge), launched by name; one-time build cost +1 min. Env switches
`UIPC_PDSB_REDUCED_SPD` / `UIPC_PDSB_BLOCKED_PROJ` / `UIPC_PDSB_TQL2` (defaults on,
all-off = the old dense 12×12 Eigen path, byte-identical registers/stack/cmem).
`make_spd.h` untouched — the helpers were already generic; only the call sites were
cold (its own lines 15-18 said so).

Transfer (recorded as a prediction, no acceptance box this round): **algorithmic**
— the projection shrinks a 12×12 dense symmetric eigenproblem to a 9×9 one in a
constant basis with a cheaper solver; same math up to rounding, nothing
grid- or occupancy-dependent (255 registers in every arm, launch mechanism
per-kernel occupancy as before). The removed work is FP64-latency-bound serial
dense algebra, so on a machine with *relatively weaker* FP64 (5090: 1/64 vs this
box's 1/32) the dense solve is an even larger share of the old kernel and the

## s02 — the dahl G/H kernel on the template machinery (pick #2)

Evidence: `agent_docs/performance/data/2026-09-15-round7-s02/` (README maps
every file). Branch `perf/round7-s02-dahl-proj` from `58a23165`.

What changed: the dahl G/H kernel gets the siblings' dispatch verbatim —
`template <int Proj, int Solver>`, six instantiations launched by name, G-only
path shared. Knobs `UIPC_DAHL_REDUCED_SPD` / `UIPC_DAHL_BLOCKED_PROJ` keep
their names, `UIPC_DAHL_TQL2` is new (mirrors `UIPC_PDSB_TQL2`); defaults
`<1,1>`, all-off `<0,0>` = the historical dense 12×12 Eigen fallback.

Two brief expectations were tested and **refuted with evidence**:

- *"`<0,0>` must match main's figures exactly, including the 11 704 B stack."*
  Impossible by construction: main's 11 704 B is the **union** of the three
  Eigen paths' frames (dense-12×12 7664 + blocked 3712 + dense-basis 6896, not
  overlapped by nvcc) — the union-of-frames cost that is the very reason the
  change is a template parameter. `<0,0>` lands at 7664 B; no instantiation
  carries 11 704 B. The old-arm behavioural identity is proved numerically
  instead (relFro equal to the family's own knob sensitivity).
- *"The Eigen→QL lever (+19.4 % on the plain hinge's projection stage)."* On
  the dahl kernel as shipped (blocked 9×9) it is worth **−0.9 %**: the inert
  stage-stub shows the projection is 74.3 % of the kernel but its eigen-solve
  core is a thin slice — the K16 block assembly dominates (110.9 ns/hinge,
  about the cost of the *entire* optimised plain-hinge kernel).

Transfer (recorded as a prediction, no acceptance box): **algorithmic +
dead-frame elimination** — the stack-frame removal is machine-independent
(occupancy/cache-layout, not grid-dependent), the solver swap is fixed-size
FP64-latency-bound dense algebra. Prediction for a rented re-measure (5090,
FP64 1/64): kernel-level −4..−6 % per launch survives; end-to-end stays
sub-1 % unless dahl's scene share grows.

Candidates found while working (for s03+, with the evidence):

1. **A Gauss-Newton dahl Hessian (the hinge's `Proj=3` analogue) — the big
   one.** Dahl's `ddEddθ = 2κw + ((M_e − s·F_c)/ℓ_e)·e^{−x} ≥ 0` always (both
   terms non-negative; `|F_c| ≤ M_e` by the commit clamp), so
   `ddEddθ·∇θ∇θᵀ` alone is PSD by construction and the `dEdθ·hess(θ)` term can
   be dropped exactly as round-6 s02 did for the plain hinge (−90 % kernel
   there). The stub measurement bounds the prize: the projection is 74.3 % of
   the kernel = 3 166 µs/launch × ~556 launches ≈ 1.76 s of GPU kernel time
   (~5 % of the scene total). Same energy, same gradient, different search
   direction — needs its own numerics/iteration-count gate like the hinge's
   `UIPC_DSB_GN_VERIFY` probe.
2. **The projection's block assembly itself** (110.9 ns/hinge across dahl,
   strain, stress, hinge — the same absolute cost everywhere): a cheaper
   assembly (e.g. folding Vdt2 into the Helmert weights, or exploiting the
   block-sparsity of the K16 sum) would pay in all four bending kernels at

3. Outside the family: `nsys` per-launch figures on this box drift ±1.2 %
   between same-binary sessions (dahl/ctl ratio 3.43-3.47 across four

## s03 — the dahl Gauss-Newton Hessian (s02's candidate #1)

Evidence: `agent_docs/performance/data/2026-09-15-round7-s03/` (README maps every
file). Branch `perf/round7-s03-dahl-gn` from `11dd35af`.

What changed: `Proj = 3` for the dahl G/H kernel — the plain hinge's round-6 s02
move, one round later. A new fused `dEdx_ddEddx_gauss_newton` computes G with
the exact fused path's own expressions (bit-identical gradient, proved by the
probe) and fills H as the mirrored rank-1 `ddEddθ·∇θ∇θᵀ` with `Vdt2` folded into
the coefficient; `dihedral_angle_hessian` is never evaluated and no eigen-solve
runs. `UIPC_DAHL_GAUSS_NEWTON=0` restores the exact Hessian + s02's knob tree;
`UIPC_DAHL_GN_VERIFY=1` is the on-device both-paths probe.

The math was verified before building (the step's first gate): over 2×200 000
randomised hinges against the real device functions — θ placed exactly by wing
rotation so d covers ±π, `F_commit` drawn by chaining the real
`commit_friction_state` (the simulator's mechanism), direct in-box, exact
boundary and fresh — **ddEddθ < 0 on 0 of 360 039 reachable samples**, min
+8.0e-6, and the minimum always sits where the analysis says it must (saturated
friction, `e^{−|d|/ℓ_e}` decayed, only the elastic `2κw` left). The split
identity `H_full = ddEddθ·ggᵀ + dEdθ·hess(θ)` reproduces to 2.0e-16, so the
extracted coefficient is the one the exact path assembles. Two documented
caveats, both handled: the brief's "[0, M_e]" guess for the legal `F_commit`
range is really the signed box **[−M_e, M_e]** (immaterial to the proof), and
the imported-history path (`dahl_friction_commit`) is validated for finiteness
only — 1.2 % of out-of-box imports would make ddEddθ negative, which the
shipped `max(·,0)` clamp absorbs at zero cost (0 activations on reachable
states). Upstream could validate the import instead; noted, not this step.

Why the wall number is smaller than the hinge's −12..−19 %: dahl was 7.2 % of
this scene's GPU kernel time (the hinge carried 12-17 % of its scenes), and
−89 % of 7.2 % ≈ −6.4 % — the measured **−6.28 % meanFrameMs at flat counts**
tracks the share almost exactly.

Transfer (recorded as a prediction, no acceptance box): **algorithmic** —
deleted FP64-latency-bound serial work at unchanged problem size and launch
count, no occupancy or grid tuning. On a machine with relatively weaker FP64
the removed share grows. Prediction for a rented re-measure (5090-class):
kernel-level −85..−92 % per launch survives; end-to-end −5..−7 % on this scene
mix; **check Newton counts first** — flat at n=16 here, but a friction-dominated
hinge's GN search direction is the one thing that could move them on another
scene, and the count question is algorithm-level, not machine-level.

Candidates found while working (for s04+, with the evidence):

1. **The dahl kernel at 16.1 ns/hinge is now the cheapest hinge in the family —
   the strain/stress plastic kernels are the outliers** (142.3 / 127.5 ns per
   hinge after s01, 4.2 % + 4.1 % of scene GPU time). Their energies are
   functions of θ alone with a **non-negative** `ddEddθ` (the round-6 V1 record
   already verified the algebra generalises: `elastic_slope` for strain, and 0
   above yield for stress — still PSD), so the same `Proj = 3` GN applies. The
   plain hinge's precedent says the prize is the whole projection + `hess(θ)`:

2. **A GN validation pass for the round** (the round-6 V1 analogue): three of
   this round's steps change search directions (s03 now, s04 if taken). The
   dahl GN's dropped term is much larger relative to its Hessian than the hinge's

   trajectory-divergence experiment with a pre-registered verdict rule would
   close the physics-equivalence question the same way V1 did.
3. Outside the family: the imported-history `dahl_friction_commit` attribute
   accepts any finite value; `do_init` could clamp or assert `|F| ≤ M_e` (the

## s04 — the plastic Gauss-Newton Hessians: REJECTED by the regime gate (2026-09-16)

Evidence: `agent_docs/performance/data/2026-09-15-round7-s04/` (README maps
every file). Branch `perf/round7-s04-plastic-gn` from main `d6f3c0bc`; the
engine tree on the branch is **identical to main** — the implementation was
built, measured and reverted, and is preserved verbatim as
`implementation.patch` (592 lines).

**The premise held.** Both plastic curvatures are non-negative on every
reachable state, proved on device exactly as s03 proved dahl (2×200 000 hinges
per kernel, θ placed by wing rotation across ±π, committed state 45 % by
chaining the real `update_plastic_state`, 35 % direct over the producible
range, 10 % fresh, 10 % out-of-range imports):

- **strain**: `ddEddθ = 2L0κ/h̄` is a *constant* — no yield-state dependence at
  all (the yield law only moves θ̄). 0 negatives in 359 009 reachable samples,
  min +1.36e-6; the `max(·,0)` clamp is inert on reachable states and absorbs
  the negative-κ import class (99.4 % of those would go negative).
- **stress**: `ddEddθ ∈ {0 exactly above yield, elastic_slope below}` — the
  response's own validity guards prove non-negativity wherever it contributes.
  0 negatives in 351 292 reachable samples, min exactly 0.0 (the designed
  yielded branch); out-of-range imports are killed by the same guards in both
  arms.

The implementation was exact: gradient bit-identical in-run (2×202 553 848
words, 0 mismatching, both kernels), split identity ≤1.72e-16, GN fills exactly
symmetric, binary whole-diff CHANGED=0/onlyBranch=3 with the new `<3,1>`
instantiations at 202 reg / 1248 B stack each (lighter than the hinge's GN
208/1248 that measured −90 %), gate identical at default and both knobs =0.

**The regime gate rejected it — per kernel, independently** (full 130-frame
`--verify` runs, one arm at a time):

| arm | Newton | PCG total | pcg_max | ls-limit | meanFrameMs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| both off (= s03 shipped), n=2 | 530-574 | 102-111k | 7-8k | 0 | 226-246 |
| both GN on | 1164 | 3 176 408 | 345 920 (stall cap) | 2 | 3026.7 |
| strain GN only | 784 | 2 066 830 | 99 070 | 0 | 1738.8 |
| stress GN only | DNF | DNF | — | — | >900 s, unfinished |

And the failures are physical, not statistical: `no_inversion_or_collapse`
trips at area ratio 0.18/29.4 (exact arms 0.25/17), strain-only lets the
clamped stack escape the z-containment limit (0.4132 > 0.41 m), the strain
yield fraction ends 5.6 % vs the 0.04-0.13 % baseline (the degraded solve
presses deeper creases — frame-0 PCG is identical across arms and the
explosion grows as yields accumulate). The exact arms' own `verify_ok` flag
flipped knife-edge checks run-to-run (`plastic_yielded` at strain 0.0 % one
draw, `no_inversion_or_collapse` at area-min 0.252 another) — the audit has
marginal checks; the iteration counts above are the non-marginal instrument,
and they are unambiguous at 19-29x.

Why the plastics fail where the hinge's and dahl's GN shipped (the
evidence-backed account): the dropped `dEdθ·hess(θ)` term is small only at the
committed working state (2 % of ‖H_full‖ at |δ| < 0.05) and dominant at the
|δ| > 1 states press-frame Newton iterates actually visit (med 0.81-1.01);
these hinges are the *stiff* constituent of the scene's 8-decade mix whose PCG
is conditioning-limited (s00's own finding), so degrading their block
multiplies the global condition — pcg_max 345 920 is a stall to the cap, the
breakdown signature the PCG-stall work documented; and the stress kernel
additionally writes exactly-zero curvature above yield, leaving fold modes
with no bending resistance at all (stress-only is the worst arm). Dahl — the
*softest* sheet, κ = 2e-5 — absorbed an identically-sized perturbation with
flat counts; stiffness mix, not perturbation size, is the differentiator.

**Consequences for the round:**

1. s03's candidate #1 is closed with numbers. The bending family's remaining
   lever is the **projection block assembly** (~110 ns/hinge in all four
   kernels, search-direction-neutral — no count risk).
2. A **curvature-floor plastic GN** (`ddEddθ ← max(ddEddθ, ε·slope)`,
   semi-smooth plasticity) is the design this rejection points at — a new
   optimization with its own premise and gates; `implementation.patch` is its
   starting point, with the probes and the isolation methodology already
   built.
3. The GN arms are a **free, reproducible PCG-stall generator** (near-singular
   crease modes on demand) for whoever hardens the linear solver next.
4. Process finding: the brief's own hedge — "the count question is
   algorithm-level" (s03's transfer note) — is now this round's measured
   truth: a PSD-by-construction Hessian with a bit-identical gradient can
   still be unusable. The regime gate is not optional paperwork for
   search-direction changes; it is the decisive one.
