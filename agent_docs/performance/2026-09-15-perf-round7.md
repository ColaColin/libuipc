# Perf round 7 — the constitutions no benchmark ever ran: dahl friction + plastic bending

Rounds 3–6 tuned the official four plus the round-6 tumbler. The TODO's own audit (PERF_METHOD §4,
"helpers that never reached their call sites") named the three shell-bending constitutions that **no
benchmark scene executes**: `DahlFrictionDiscreteShellBending` (zero callers in the repo at all; used
by `cloth-dataset`/`cloth-machine` outside it), `StrainPlasticDiscreteShellBending` and
`StressPlasticDiscreteShellBending` (C++ sim_case correctness tests only). A same-day coverage
survey confirmed only 5 of ~25 constitution families have benchmark callers, and that these three
kernels are the least-optimized in the bending family:

- the plain hinge ships `<Proj = 3 Gauss-Newton, Solver = 1 QL>` (round 6 s02/s19: −90 % kernel,
  −12 to −19 % end-to-end);
- dahl still launches with **runtime bools** and `Solver = 0` — Eigen's `SelfAdjointEigenSolver`,
  11 704 B stack frame, 255 registers, the largest in the family;
- the plastic variants call the bare dense `make_spd(H12x12)` — the +81.0 % / +19.4 % levers
  measured on the plain hinge's dense path apply to them verbatim.

Round 7 changes the workload again, in the TODO's own suggested shape: a **cyclic press creasing a
mixed stack** — denim (dahl), cardboard (strain-plastic), sheet metal (stress-plastic) — so those
kernels carry real benchmark load, then spends ten sequential steps wiring the proven hinge
machinery into the call sites that never had it. `NeoHookeanShell2D`, also unrepresented, is the
membrane for the carton/metal sheets.

## Survey findings that fixed the scene choice (2026-09-15)

Constitution coverage matrix and the full unrepresented-parts ranking live in
`data/2026-09-15-round7-survey/`. Decisions taken from it:

1. **The scene targets the three plastic/frictional bending constitutions** — the only family where
   the TODO could quote measured headroom (+81.0 % dense-solve, +19.4 % `Solver=0`, −90 % GN
   analogue) with no scene to spend it on.
2. `NeoHookeanShell2D` is the membrane for the carton/metal sheets — a second unrepresented kernel
   exercised at no extra scene cost. Denim keeps `StrainLimitingBaraffWitkinShell`, matching the
   dahl users in `cloth-dataset`.
3. **Legacy broad-phase filters** (`lbvh`, `stackless_bvh`, `info_stackless_bvh_v0`): runtime-
   selectable via `collision_detection/method`, no benchmark sets it, one example (33) does. Round-6
   s04/s06 never reached them, but there is no benchmark justification to optimize them either —
   the TODO item is answered "reachable but unloaded; not this round" rather than deleted.
4. **Pipeline-level gaps** (AL-IPC contact constitution, ABD joints, BDF2, kirchhoff rods, vertex
   stitches, plain PCG) are future workload candidates, not one scene's payload — changing them
   changes what the scene *is*.

## s00 — the instrument

Scene: `libuipc-samples/examples/103_crease_press/` (next free number after
`102_net`). Evidence: `agent_docs/performance/data/2026-09-15-round7-s00/`.

**What it is.** A cyclic hydraulic press creasing a mixed stack of seven clamped
sheets — 3 denim (`StrainLimitingBaraffWitkinShell` + `DahlFrictionDiscreteShellBending`,
the cloth-dataset towel values verbatim: kappa 2e-5, ell 0.3, m_hat 8e-3, 0.8 mm,
0.30 kg/m², finest mesh), 2 cardboard (`NeoHookeanShell` + `StrainPlasticDiscreteShellBending`)
and 2 sheet metal (`NeoHookeanShell` + `StressPlasticDiscreteShellBending`),
101_press values with the yield thresholds carried over as the same yield
*curvature* (0.02 rad on 50 mm elements → 0.006 rad on 15.5 mm; the stress
threshold 250 stays verbatim and lands at the same per-hinge angle). The denim
is interleaved through the stack. One kinematic ABD press bar (cube +
`SoftTransformConstraint` + animator, 101_press's smooth schedule) runs **two
press/hold/lift cycles at two lateral offsets** (z = ±80 mm) with a lateral
shift between them, so creases form in two places and the dahl hinges run
hysteresis loops; the ground halfplane is the die that arrests the fold.
Everything is procedural; no new binary assets. 130 frames at dt = 1/60
(calibrated down from 101_press's 0.01 — at dt 0.01 the soft denim needs >10
Newton iterations per press frame and the run costs 3x the wall budget).

**Size.** 47,569 bending hinges (28,557 dahl / 9,506 strain-plastic / 9,506
stress-plastic), 32,168 triangles, 16,774 vertices; 35-39 s per 130-frame run
(n=5 mean 37.2 s), peak 2.47-2.72 GiB GPU (nvidia-smi total delta — the brief's
< 2.5 GB target, met at the 2.5 GiB line). `size_sweep.md` records the
dt/tolerance sweep, the element-size table (the carton edge is bounded below by
the contact-resolution rule applied to its 2.5 mm one-sided thickness: 14.2 mm
minimum), and the **five press-geometry drafts** it took to make offset creases
on a clamped stack work at all — four traps for the next scene of this kind:
no die under an offset crease → the yielded fold swings past vertical; die +
deep press → the clamp-corner triangles of the stiff carton shear to 30x rest
area along the sqrt(span²+depth²) membrane draw; kiss-press → a broad elastic
dome that never yields (the carton's bending boundary layer is metres wide);
die too shallow → contained but dead. The shipped geometry (die 75 mm under the
rest plane, face travel 0.10 m below the stack's rest top, sheet 0.48x0.80 m)
is the combination that localises the kink without wrecking the corners.

**Regime (mandatory check 2), measured by replicating the engine's per-frame
commit kernels in numpy** (`verify.py` tracks dahl `F_commit` with the exact
device update and both plastic `theta_bar` laws, hinge set for hinge set — the
hinge census of a flat grid sheet is its interior edges):

| observable | value |
|---|---|
| dahl `F_commit` non-zero fraction after press 1 | 0.9996 of hinges |
| dahl \|F\|/M at the crease lines, final | 0.064-0.067 of saturation |
| stress-plastic hinges holding a permanent `theta_bar` | 2.2-3.4 % |
| strain-plastic hinges holding a permanent `theta_bar` | 0.04-0.13 % (the kink peaks) |
| residual crease after retract, per sheet (7 sheets) | 37-48 mm on **every** sheet |

The strain fraction is small because the crease kink concentrates on 2-4 hinges
per crease line while the audit's yield fraction is over all 9,506 hinges; the
kernels' load does not depend on it (same launch geometry either way).

**Soundness** (`--verify`, full 130-frame default run — `verify_run_130f.txt`):

| check | number |
|---|---|

### Noise floor

n=5 full runs (`noise_floor.txt`): `meanFrameMs` cv **4.34 %**, `ms_per_newton`
cv **2.69 %** (the tightest statistic again), `newton_total` cv 2.56 %,
`pcg_total` cv 7.64 % — and **`medianFrameMs` cv 20.6 %, worthless**: the
workload is bimodal (47 press/shift frames at 400-1200 ms against a settle tail
at 40-80 ms), so the median lands in the gap and jumps between arms. Later
steps must gate on `meanFrameMs` or `ms_per_newton`, never the median, on this
scene. The scene is non-deterministic (first PCG difference at frame 0 — the
7-layer pre-contact stack's first solve is chaotic; the tumbler's was frame 4).
Min detectable effect at n=5/arm: 5.4 % on `meanFrameMs`, 3.4 % on
`ms_per_newton`; n/arm for a 2 % effect: 37 / 15.

### First kernel ranking (nsys, full 130-frame run, fresh prefix)

Family shares of 34.7 s total GPU kernel time:

| family | share | total |
|---|---|---|
| PCG kernels (spmv/dot/update) | 26.1 % | 9083 ms |
| contact assemble (`do_assemble`) | 14.6 % | 5071 ms |
| BVH + CCD (`InfoStacklessBVH*`) | 12.9 % | 4481 ms |
| preconditioners (MAS + abd_diag) | 12.5 % | 4346 ms |
| **dahl bending G/H+energy** | **7.2 %** | 2498 ms |
| converter/sort/compact | 7.3 % | 2534 ms |
| **strain-plastic bending** | **4.2 %** | 1469 ms |
| **stress-plastic bending** | **4.1 %** | 1433 ms |
| membrane (SLBW + NeoHookean2D) | 5.3 % | 1841 ms |

Top individual kernels: SpMV 20.3 % (66.1 µs × 106,560), `do_assemble<k1>`
8.7 %, MAS local solve 7.3 %, **dahl G/H 7.0 % (4401 µs × 556)**,
**strain G/H 4.1 % (2550 µs × 556)**, **stress G/H 4.0 % (2518 µs × 556)**,
NeoHookean2D G/H 3.8 %. The three target kernels: 582→556 launches each (one
per Newton iteration), **15.4 % of GPU kernel time combined**, and the dahl
`TimeIntegrator` commit kernel launches exactly 130 times — once per frame —
as required. Per hinge: dahl 154 ns, strain 268 ns, stress 265 ns — the plain
hinge after round-6 s02 runs 128-145 ns/hinge, so the plastic variants carry
~1.9x the optimized plain hinge's per-hinge cost and have none of the s02/s14/s19
machinery.

### Pick list for s01+ (ranked by measured share)

1. **The plastic-bending PSD projection / dense solve** (`make_spd(H12x12)` in
   both plastic kernels): the two kernels are 8.3 % combined at ~266 ns/hinge
   against the plain hinge's post-s02 128-145 ns/hinge. Round-6's s14/s19
   levers (+81 % dense-solve / +19.4 % `Solver=0` on the plain hinge) were
   never wired into these call sites. A GN-analogue or blocked projection that
   halves per-hinge cost is worth ~4 % of this scene's GPU time and is
   measurable end-to-end at n=5 (floor 5.4 % on `meanFrameMs` — borderline;
   gate on the per-kernel scope).
2. **The dahl G/H kernel** (7.0 %, the largest constitution kernel in the
   suite): 255 registers, 11.7 kB stack, `Solver = 0` (Eigen
   `SelfAdjointEigenSolver`) and runtime bools — the exact profile the round
   was opened with. The K7 blocked projection already ships behind runtime
   bools; templating them and applying s02's GN-style Hessian is the same shape
   of work as (1) on a bigger kernel.
3. **PCG/SpmV** (26 %): not bending-family, but the biggest single family —
   mostly a conditioning problem (the stack mixes stiffnesses 8 decades apart),
   not a kernel problem; the round's env-switch discipline applies if touched.
4. **Contact assembly** (14.6 %) and **BVH+CCD** (12.9 %): round-6 s06/s07/s08
   already landed the big wins there; these are the regression checks more
   than candidates.
